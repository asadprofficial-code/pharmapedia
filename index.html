PharmaPedia Match

Demonstration · A 2D pharmacy learning game

Disclaimer: Student project for the Pharm-D, IT lab. Not medical advice.

How to play

  1. You get 15 rounds, one for each medicine.
  2. Match each medicine with its drug class, or find the medicine that belongs to a class.
  3. Pick one of 4 answers. You see right or wrong straight away, with the explanation.
  4. +10 for a correct answer, +5 bonus from a streak of 3 or more.

No login. No sign-up. Nothing you do is saved or sent anywhere.

Study Cards

Tap a card to flip it. Learn all 15, then start the game.
